Privacy & Cookies Policy
Last Updated: 29/7/2026
Welcome to Meroestream. We believe in the power of stories and the integrity of the people who tell them. This extends to how we handle your data. This Privacy and Cookies Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect your privacy while you engage with our platform.
1. What Information We Collect
We collect information to help us better understand our community and improve the Meroestream experience. This generally falls into two categories:
Information You Provide Voluntarily: When you contact us, sign up for a newsletter, or submit an inquiry, we may collect your name, email address, and any other information you choose to share with us.
Information Collected Automatically: As you browse meroestream.com, our systems may automatically collect technical data, including your IP address, browser type, device information, and browsing activity (such as which pages you visit and for how long).
2. How We Use Your Data
We are transparent about why we collect your data:
To Connect & Communicate: To respond to your inquiries, talent submissions, or feedback.
To Improve Our Platform: To analyze usage trends so we can build a better home for African storytelling.
To Maintain Security: To protect our platform, its users, and our intellectual property from malicious activity.
We do not sell your personal data. We are building a community, not a data-mining operation.
3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies
We use cookies; small files stored on your device, to help our website function and to give you a personalized experience.
Essential Cookies: These are necessary for the website to function (e.g., security and site navigation). You cannot opt out of these.
Performance & Analytics Cookies: These help us understand how our community interacts with our content (e.g., Google Analytics). They help us see what is resonating so we can bring you more of it.
Functionality Cookies: These remember your preferences, such as your language or region.
How to Manage Cookies: You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Please note that if you choose to block all cookies, some features of our website may not work as intended.
