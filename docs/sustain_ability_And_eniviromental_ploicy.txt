Sustainability And Environmental Policy: Meroestream
This Environmental Policy is designed specifically for Meroestream. It covers the standard sustainability requirements for film, digital, and media production, aligning our commitment to independent storytelling with respect for our planet.
1. Vision and Commitment
Meroestream recognizes that film and digital media production carry an environmental footprint through energy consumption, travel, resource use, and waste generation. We believe that empowering African storytelling and preserving the environment go hand-in-hand. We are committed to minimizing our ecological impact and contributing to a sustainable future for the global creative industry.
Our goal is to integrate environmental responsibility into every stage of the creative and production process—from script to screen—ensuring that telling our stories does not come at the expense of our planet.
2. Core Objectives
We aim to:
Comply with all relevant local and international environmental regulations and align with sustainable media frameworks (such as albert or the Green Production Guide).
Reduce our total carbon footprint by prioritizing energy efficiency, digital-first workflows, and low-carbon transport.
Minimize waste across all operations through the principles of "Reduce, Reuse, Recycle."
Promote environmental awareness, sustainability, and green production practices among our team, filmmakers, freelancers, and creative partners.
3. Production Guidelines (On-Set & In-Field)
To achieve these objectives, Meroestream encourages and implements the following practices during physical production and content creation:
A. Energy & Lighting
LED First: We prioritize energy-efficient LED lighting systems over traditional high-energy lamps across all set setups and shoots.
Smart Power Sources: We utilize mains grid power whenever available. Where generators are required, we prioritize hybrid systems, battery setups, or clean fuel alternatives (e.g., HVO/biodiesel).
Power Management: All production equipment, monitors, and lighting setups must be powered down during breaks and non-active hours.
B. Travel & Transport
Vehicle Efficiency: We prioritize low-emission, hybrid, or electric vehicles for crew transport and local logistics.
Consolidated Travel: We encourage carpooling, shared crew transport, and public transit for office staff, production crews, and remote teams.
Flight Reduction: Flights are reserved for essential travel only. When air travel is necessary, direct routes are prioritized, and carbon offsetting options are evaluated.
C. Catering & Craft Service
Zero Single-Use Plastics: Meroestream sets are single-use plastic-free zones. We provide water refill stations and require all crew and talent to use reusable bottles and mugs.
Waste Management: Clear, labeled stations are set up on location for recycling, composting/organic waste, and general disposal.
Local Sourcing: We prioritize local, seasonal caterers and support plant-based (vegetarian/vegan) menu choices to reduce the environmental footprint of food supply chains.
D. Art Department & Set Operations
Sustainable Sourcing: We favor sustainably sourced materials, local crafts, FSC-certified timber, and eco-friendly or non-toxic paints for set design and staging.
Responsible Strike Plans: Before sets are constructed, a wrap/strike plan must be established to ensure materials are repurposed, donated to local creative spaces, or recycled rather than sent to landfills.
4. Digital & Office Operations
Digital-First Workflow: Meroestream operates on a digital-first model. Scripts, call sheets, schedules, contracts, and press kits are shared and processed digitally to minimize paper use.
Remote Efficiency: Where possible, pre-production meetings, story reviews, and administrative operations are conducted virtually to limit unnecessary commuting.
Energy Management: Our office and post-production setups utilize energy-saving hardware and support renewable energy providers whenever possible.
5. Procurement and Strategic Partners
Meroestream will:
Favor Eco-Conscious Vendors: Partner with suppliers, rental houses, and service providers who maintain active environmental policies or green certifications.
Distribute Green Memos: Share our sustainability guidelines with production partners, heads of departments, and collaborative teams during pre-production.
6. Accountability & Ongoing Improvement
For major productions and platform initiatives, Meroestream will endeavor to:
Assign a Green Lead / Sustainability Steward to oversee policy execution on set and in production offices.
Measure production carbon footprints using recognized media industry calculators (e.g., the albert calculator).
Review and update this policy annually to incorporate new eco-friendly technologies, local sustainable resources, and evolving best practices.
